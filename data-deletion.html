User Data Deletion

Blazing Bakers · Last updated: 13 November 2026

You can ask Blazing Bakers to delete the personal information we hold about you from our WhatsApp ordering service. This page explains how, what is deleted and how long it takes. For what we collect and why, see our Privacy Policy.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us at poshambiz@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data. Include the WhatsApp phone number you used to message us.
  2. Or message us on WhatsApp: choose Talk to our team and say "delete my data".

To protect your information, we confirm the request by replying on the same WhatsApp number or email address before deleting anything.

What we delete

  • Your customer record in our spreadsheet: phone number, name, chat history markers and opt-out status.
  • Your order and enquiry entries, including details and photos or locations you sent for orders.
  • Staff alert messages about you that we control.

How long it takes

We complete deletion within 30 days of confirming your request and tell you when it is done. Automation logs on our own computer already expire after 7 days.

What may remain

  • Records we must keep by law, such as invoices for completed orders, for the period the law requires.
  • Backup copies, which are replaced on a rolling basis; deleted information is not restored from backups.
  • Your messages inside your own WhatsApp app and on Meta's systems. We cannot delete those. WhatsApp and Meta handle them under their own terms and tools.
  • Text you sent in a free-form question that was processed by Google Gemini. We do not store it there. Google handles it under its own terms.

Facebook and Meta accounts

Our service does not use Facebook Login and does not store data from your Facebook account. We receive only what you send us on WhatsApp.
