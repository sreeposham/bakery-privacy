Privacy Policy

Blazing Bakers · Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Blazing Bakers ("we", "us") collects when you message our bakery on WhatsApp, how we use it, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By starting a WhatsApp chat with us you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Blazing Bakers, 201, Gayathri Apartment, Serilingampally, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. Contact for privacy questions: poshambiz@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • WhatsApp details: your phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • Your messages: the text you send, and any photos, documents or locations you share (for example a cake design reference or a delivery location).
  • Order and enquiry details: items, quantities, dates, pickup or delivery address, and notes you give us.
  • Chat records: when you first and last messaged us, how many messages, the stage of your chat (for example "waiting for order details"), and whether you asked us to stop messages.

We do not ask for payment card numbers, passwords or government ID through WhatsApp. Please do not send them.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to you, show our menu, take and confirm orders and custom cake requests, and arrange pickup or delivery.
  • To let our staff follow up when you ask for a person or send order details.
  • To keep our service running and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Automated and AI replies

Our WhatsApp assistant replies automatically. For free-text questions it uses an AI service (Google Gemini) to draft an answer from our shop information. Only the text of your question is sent for this; your phone number and name are not. AI answers can be wrong, so they are never an order confirmation: our staff confirm orders, prices and availability. Ask for "our team" at any time to talk to a person.

5. Who handles your information

Service providerWhat it does

Google (Sheets, Gemini)Sheets stores our customer and order records. Gemini receives the text of free-form questions, as described above. Under Google's free developer terms, content sent to the service may be used by Google to improve its products.

These providers act for us and have their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Automation logs on our computer are deleted automatically after 7 days. Customer and order records in our spreadsheet are kept for up to 24 months after your last message, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires us to keep them longer (for example for tax records).

7. Your choices and rights

  • Stop messages: reply STOP in the chat and we will mark you as opted out.
  • Access, correction or deletion: email poshambiz@gmail.com from, or mentioning, the WhatsApp number you used. We will respond within 30 days.
  • Data deletion: see how to request deletion. A deletion request removes your record from our spreadsheet and any alerts we control. Messages inside your own WhatsApp app stay on your phone and are governed by WhatsApp.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under applicable law (including, in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). Contact us to use them.

8. Security

Access to our records is limited to our staff, our systems use encrypted connections, and credentials are stored in our automation tool rather than in shared files. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Our WhatsApp service is for adults and young people ordering with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18 without parental consent.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
